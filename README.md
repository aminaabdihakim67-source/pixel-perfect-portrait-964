# ProofPath
### Your Skills Deserve to Be Seen.

**ProofPath** is an early-stage digital platform designed to help skilled refugees make their professional abilities visible to employers, mentors and training providers.

Our initial focus is on skilled refugees in **Dadaab Refugee Complex, Garissa County, Kenya**, including Dadaab, Ifo and Dagahaley.

## The Problem

Many displaced professionals have valuable education, practical skills and work experience but face barriers when their qualifications are unavailable, unfamiliar or difficult to recognise.

This can lead to brain waste: skilled people being unable to use their abilities in meaningful employment.

## Our Solution

ProofPath helps users organise their professional experience, identify transferable competencies, demonstrate practical skills and create a structured **Evidence Passport**.

## Key Features

- **Bilingual interface:** English and Af-Soomaali.
- **Skills profiles:** Organise education, professional experience and skills.
- **Experience-to-skills translator:** Discover transferable competencies from previous work.
- **Practical assessments:** Demonstrate abilities in teaching, digital administration, translation and basic IT support.
- **Evidence Passport:** Generate a printable summary of skills, experience and assessment results.
- **Employer and mentor portal:** Explore demonstration profiles and review skills evidence.

## Target Community

Skilled refugees in Dadaab, Ifo and Dagahaley, including educators, healthcare professionals, translators, IT technicians, administrators, tradespeople and entrepreneurs.

## Important Disclaimer

ProofPath is an early-stage prototype developed for demonstration purposes. Information entered is stored locally in this browser. The platform helps users organise and demonstrate skills but does not independently verify qualifications, issue certifications or guarantee employment opportunities.
